NotiHop forwards notifications one-way from a <b>sender</b> phone to a <b>receiver</b> phone over a direct, paired Classic Bluetooth (RFCOMM/SPP) connection. Both phones install the same app; you pick the role in Settings. No internet is used and no data ever leaves your Bluetooth range.

The sender captures its own notifications via Android's Notification Listener and transmits them; the receiver posts them as local notifications. Messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS) are reconstructed as proper conversation notifications, so a new message updates the existing conversation rather than spamming you.

Features:

- <b>App filter</b> — choose which apps' notifications are forwarded. New apps can be enabled automatically (configurable).
- <b>Phone call forwarding</b> — optionally forward incoming calls as notifications, with the caller's name resolved from your local contacts.
- <b>Autostart</b> — optionally restart the service after a reboot.
- <b>Connect-on-demand</b> — the sender connects only when there is something to send, then disconnects, to save battery.

Privacy:

- No internet permission at all. Everything stays on your two devices over Bluetooth.
- No analytics, no ads, no third-party trackers, no crash reporting.
- Contacts and the incoming-call number are read only on the sender, only when call forwarding is enabled, and only the resolved caller name is transmitted.
- The only thing stored on-device is your settings (role, paired device address, filters).

Permissions: Bluetooth (connect/scan), notification posting, a foreground service, boot completion, battery-optimisation exemption, and (for the optional call-forwarding feature) phone state, call log and contacts.

NotiHop is free and open source (MIT). Both devices must be owned by the same user and support Classic Bluetooth. Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer.
